Boker & Bedtime Shema

Offline Hebrew morning prayers and bedtime Shema for iPhone.

Reading your prayers

The app opens morning prayers during your chosen daytime hours and bedtime Shema in the evening. Use the sun and moon buttons to switch at any time. Tap the prayer list to jump to a section.

Tap AA to choose a Hebrew font, change text size or appearance, and set when morning and bedtime begin. Your preferences are saved on your iPhone. These schedule preferences are not halachic prayer times.

Bedtime Shema uses the Sefard bedtime berachah and order with all three paragraphs of Shema from Siddur Ashkenaz. Divine names are abbreviated throughout.

Support and feedback

Send a support request or report a text correction. Please include your iOS version, app version and a description of the issue. Please do not include personal or sensitive information in a public support request.
