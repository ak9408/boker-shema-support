Privacy policy

Boker & Bedtime Shema for iPhone
Effective October 30, 2026

Data collected by the app

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It contains no advertising, tracking, analytics or account registration. All prayer texts and fonts are bundled on your iPhone for offline reading.

Preferences on your device

Your selected font, text size, appearance and reading schedule are stored locally on your iPhone. The app reads your device’s local clock to choose which prayer collection to show. It does not request your location.

External links and support

If you choose to open a text source, a license link or this support website, the destination website’s privacy practices apply. The support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. If you choose to submit a support request through GitHub, the information you submit is handled by GitHub and may be public. Do not include personal or sensitive information in a public request.

Apple services

Apple may process App Store, TestFlight, device diagnostic or purchase-related information under Apple’s privacy policy and your device settings. The app contains no third-party diagnostic or tracking SDK.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support page.
